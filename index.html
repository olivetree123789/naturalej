About Naturalej

The Royal Principality of Naturalej, commonly known as Naturalej, is a micronation with designated Novas located in Poland and the United States.

Naturalej is organized as a Constitutional Democratic Principality and is headed by Prince Oliver Komasara.

The designated capital of Naturalej is Komorów, Poland. The official languages are Polish and English.

Naturalej is a micronation and does not claim internationally recognized sovereignty over the areas associated with its Novas.

Government

The Royal Principality of Naturalej operates as a Constitutional Democratic Principality.

The Prince

The Prince is the head of state of Naturalej. The position is held by Prince Oliver Komasara.

Grand Council

The Grand Council may propose and debate legislation and participate in decisions concerning the administration of Naturalej.

Council of Elders

The Council of Elders provides advice, guidance, and deliberation within the governmental structure of the Principality.

A bill does not become official law until it is signed by the Prince.

Novas

The first-level administrative divisions of Naturalej are known as Novas. Naturalej currently has four Novas.

Nova Komorowa

Location: Komorów, Poland

Nova Komorowa contains the designated capital of Naturalej and has a special connection to the Crown.

Nova Busko-Zdroja

Location: Busko-Zdrój, Poland

Nova Busko-Zdroja reflects Polish cultural identity and the area's association with mineral and healing waters.

Nova Mount Prospecta

Location: Mount Prospect, Illinois, United States

Nova Mount Prospecta is the Nova where the Prince is most often present.

Nova Glenova

Location: Glenview, Illinois, United States

Nova Glenova emphasizes nature, greenery, growth, peace, and harmony.

National Symbols

The Royal Flag of Naturalej

The Royal Flag of Naturalej features a light green background with a red apple at its center containing a white heart.

The apple represents nature and growth, while the white heart represents a clear heart, unity, and love of nature.

Coat of Arms

The coat of arms of Naturalej includes the red apple and white heart, a crown representing the Principality and Crown, and imagery representing the Principality's connections to Poland, the United States, and nature.

National Motto

United by Nature, Towards a Brighter Future.

National Anthem

The national anthem of the Royal Principality of Naturalej is United by Nature.

NRM Currency

The official micronational currency of Naturalej is the NRM.

The NRM uses the symbol ₦, an N with a horizontal line through its center. The symbol is placed before the amount.

History

The ideas, national symbols, institutions, and governmental structure of Naturalej developed before the establishment of the present constitutional system.

An earlier constitutional document was created on 20 July 2025 during the early development of Naturalej.

During 2026, the governmental structure, Novas, national symbols, currency, and constitutional system were further developed.

The present Royal Principality of Naturalej is planned to be formally established on 5 September 2026, when its present Constitution is scheduled to enter into force.

Constitution

The Constitution of Naturalej establishes the structure and principles of the Royal Principality, including the Crown, the Prince, the Grand Council, the Council of Elders, individual rights, the Novas, citizenship, national symbols, finance, succession, and constitutional amendments.

The Constitution is intended to serve as the highest internal law of Naturalej.
